Mostly one thing: AI voice agents that talk to real customers, and everything that has to work around them.

Now · since 03/2026

Working student, AI applications

I work hands-on on production voice agents built on Leaping, handling real customer calls. I look at what actually happens on calls, then change how the agents and the workflows around them behave.

  • Routing, prompts and conversation behaviour, function/tool nodes and field logic in Leaping.
  • Customer verification, API/CRM-backed actions, tickets and transfers, checked against what the functions actually did.
  • Outbound flows, including a scheduling path that books through a tool and only confirms on success.
  • Production call audits, failure analysis, QA categories and regression cases before changes ship.
