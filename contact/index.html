Have something messy that should be a system?

If you're building something interesting, have an operational process that needs fixing, or think we'd build well together, tell me about it.

Founders, small teams, other builders, open-source people and anyone with an odd experimental idea: all equally welcome.

A good first message

  1. 01
    Your name

    And how to reach you, if not by replying.

  2. 02
    What you're trying to build or fix

    Roughly how it works today, which tools are involved, and where it breaks.

  3. 03
    A link

    Optional: a repo, a doc, a screenshot, anything that helps.
