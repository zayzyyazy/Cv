§ 02 · Production work

The conversation is the easy part.

Since early 2026 I've worked as a working student on MARIE, an AI voice agent handling real customer calls in production.

The main thing it taught me: the hard part of an AI agent isn't what it says. It's everything around it. Which tool fires, what gets written to the CRM, what happens after the call ends, and how you notice when one of those quietly fails.

Details are confidential, so there are no screenshots here.

§ 03 · How I decide

AI where it's useful. Rules where they're needed. People where it matters.

Not every step needs a model. Pick a case and see where it goes. All of them come from the projects above.

Unstructured / ambiguous

→ AI

Interpretation, classification, summarizing language.

Known rule

→ Code

Calculations, thresholds, validation, state changes.

Uncertain / high-risk

→ A person

Exceptions, policy-sensitive calls, anything that needs responsibility.

A bit about me

I'm Zara. I study Human-Centered Informatics & Psychology and spend most of my time building things: workflows, small tools and a lot of experiments, most of them on GitHub.

I'm still growing on the deeper backend side: databases, deployment, running things long-term. What I can already do is take a messy process, figure out how it really works, and build something that handles it.

  • I like understanding messy systems before touching them.
  • I care about what happens when the automation fails.
  • I test with real workflows, not just the happy path.
  • I'd rather build useful AI than impressive-looking AI.
