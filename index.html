How I build

Start with the process, then decide where AI belongs.

I do not start from "let's use a model." I start by understanding the work, connecting the systems, defining what can be automated safely and deciding where humans need to stay in the loop.

1. Understand the process

Map what happens today, where information enters, who touches it and where things slow down or break.

2. Connect the systems

Use APIs, webhooks and workflow tools to move data and actions between services instead of copying by hand.

3. Handle the exceptions

Combine rules, AI steps and review paths so the system knows what to do when information is missing or uncertain.
