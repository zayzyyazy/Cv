How I decide

AI is one component in the system.

The strongest systems are not "all AI". They mix models, deterministic code, business rules, storage and human judgment.

Deterministic logic

Use for rules, calculations, validation, known constraints, order of operations and safety-critical state transitions.

AI

Use for interpretation, classification, summarization, recommendations and unstructured language where strict rules are not enough.

Human review

Use for ambiguity, policy-sensitive outcomes, exceptions and cases where the right answer needs responsibility, not just probability.
